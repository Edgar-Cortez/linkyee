Why Transdermal

Most people are magnesium-deficient — and pills are hard on the body that needs it most.

Oral magnesium has to survive digestion before it ever reaches tired muscle or an unsettled nervous system, and for a lot of people that trip ends in stomach upset before it ends in relief. Applied to skin, magnesium bypasses the gut entirely — absorbed close to where it's needed, without the trade-off.

Bypasses digestion

No capsules to metabolize, no gut lining to irritate — the mineral goes on where you feel it.

Targeted, not systemic

Apply directly to a sore shoulder, tight calves, or the base of the neck — relief where you actually need it.

Fits real routines

A spray after a workout, a gel before bed, a cream worked in during a five-minute pause — no water, no timing it around meals.

Our Story

Built at our kitchen counter, for one person first.

Sol Salt started as something much smaller than a brand — a husband trying to find real relief for his wife through pregnancy and postpartum recovery, when so much of what was out there either didn't work or wasn't gentle enough to trust on her skin.

What we landed on became Sol Salt: magnesium made simply, in small batches, by the two of us — still tested on ourselves before it ever reaches a shelf.

— Edgar & Julie, co-founders
